📖 Surah Al-Baqarah

About Surah Al-Baqarah:

Surah Al-Baqarah is the longest Surah of the Holy Quran. It provides guidance on faith, worship, law, and daily life.

الم
ذَٰلِكَ الْكِتَابُ لَا رَيْبَ ۛ فِيهِ ۛ هُدًى لِلْمُتَّقِينَ
الَّذِينَ يُؤْمِنُونَ بِالْغَيْبِ وَيُقِيمُونَ الصَّلَاةَ

Translation:
Alif, Lam, Meem. This is the Book about which there is no doubt, a guidance for those conscious of Allah, who believe in the unseen and establish prayer.
