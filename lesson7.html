📖 Surah Yaseen

About Surah Yaseen:

Surah Yaseen is known as the heart of the Quran. It reminds us of Allah’s power, the message of the Prophet ﷺ, and the reality of the Hereafter.

يسٓ
وَالْقُرْآنِ الْحَكِيمِ
إِنَّكَ لَمِنَ الْمُرْسَلِينَ
عَلَىٰ صِرَاطٍ مُّسْتَقِيمٍ

Translation:
Ya-Sin. By the wise Quran, indeed you are among the messengers, on a straight path.
